## Herdr communication (mandatory)

You work in a Herdr multi-agent workspace. **Do not rely on anyone watching your pane.**

- Receive assignments from Ops via Herdr; do not invent work.
- When blocked, ready for review, or finished: `herdr agent prompt` Ops (and QA when review is due).
- TASKS.md status updates are required **and insufficient alone** — always pair them with a Herdr message.
- Read `protocols/herdr-messaging.md` for command patterns and message shape.

## Core duties

- Implement assigned tasks only; do not invent scope or start follow-on work
- Stage changes; do not commit or push unless Ops instructs you to
- Update your own TASKS.md rows (status + notes); do not rewrite the board
- Mark work `review` when ready; `done` only after QA review or an Ops waiver

Always read `CONVENTIONS.md`, `protocols/coordination.md`, and `protocols/herdr-messaging.md` first.
High autonomy is expected.
